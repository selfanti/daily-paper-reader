## 摘要
本文研究了在Wang等人(2020)、Jia和Zhou(2022b)提出的连续时间强化学习框架下，由随机微分方程(SDE)描述的扩散环境中的多臂老虎机问题的策略梯度更新。对于采用logit参数化的随机策略，我们证明在任意常数学习率下，该策略几乎必然收敛到最优臂。此外，当常数学习率低于一个不随时间变化的阈值时，我们推导出非渐近遗憾上界，且遗憾界阶为O(log T)。我们通过构造一个新的Lyapunov函数，改进了Lattimore(2026a)对同一SDE的分析，并展示了使用SDE工具分析策略梯度的透明性。此外，同一个Lyapunov函数也有助于分析离散时间策略梯度算法。

## Abstract
This paper studies the policy gradient update for a multi-arm bandit problem in diffusion environment that is described by a stochastic differential equation (SDE) under the continuous-time reinforcement learning framework by Wang et al. (2020), Jia and Zhou (2022b). With the logit parameterization for the stochastic policy, we show that it converges almost surely to the optimal arm under an arbitrary constant learning rate. Furthermore, we derive the non-asymptotic regret upper bound when the constant learning rate is below a time-invariant threshold; and the regret bound has order $O(\log T)$. We improve the analysis in Lattimore (2026a) for the same SDE by constructing a novel Lyapunov function and demonstrate the transparency of analyzing policy gradient using the tools in SDEs. In addition, the same Lyapunov function is also helpful in analyzing the discrete-time policy gradient algorithm.

### 2. 方法论：核心思想、关键技术细节

#### 2.1 问题设定与核心模型

- **连续时间强化学习框架**（Wang et al. 2020, Jia and Zhou 2022b）：将MAB建模为随机控制问题，臂 $a$ 的累积奖励满足 $dR_t^{(a)} = \mu^{(a)}dt + \sigma^{(a)}dB_t^{(a)}$。
- **策略参数化**：采用对数线性（Logit）参数化，即 $\pi^{(a)}(\boldsymbol{\phi}) = \frac{\exp(\phi^{(a)})}{\sum_{j=1}^d \exp(\phi^{(j)})}$。
- **核心SDE（式2）**：在常数学习率下，策略参数满足：
  $$d\boldsymbol{\phi}_t = \ell \boldsymbol{J}(\boldsymbol{\pi}_t)\boldsymbol{\mu}\,dt + \ell\boldsymbol{G}_{\boldsymbol{\sigma}}(\boldsymbol{\pi}_t)\,d\boldsymbol{B}^{\phi}_t$$
  其中 $\boldsymbol{J}(\boldsymbol{\pi}) = \operatorname{diag}\{\boldsymbol{\pi}\} - \boldsymbol{\pi}\boldsymbol{\pi}^\top$，$\boldsymbol{G}_{\boldsymbol{\sigma}}(\boldsymbol{\pi})$ 是依赖于策略和噪声的扩散矩阵。

#### 2.2 关键数学工具：Lyapunov函数方法

论文的核心技术贡献是构造了一个**新颖的Lyapunov函数**，用于证明收敛性和推导遗憾界：

$$V_{\ell,\boldsymbol{\sigma}}(\boldsymbol{\phi}) := \sum_{\emptyset \neq A \subseteq I} C_{\ell,\boldsymbol{\sigma}}^{|A|-1} h_A(\boldsymbol{\phi}) = \frac{1}{C_{\ell,\boldsymbol{\sigma}}}\left[\prod_{i=2}^d\left(1 + C_{\ell,\boldsymbol{\sigma}} e^{\phi^{(i)}-\phi^{(1)}}\right) - 1\right]$$

其中 $C_{\ell,\boldsymbol{\sigma}} = K_{\ell,\boldsymbol{\sigma}}/c_{\ell,\boldsymbol{\sigma}}$，$K_{\ell,\boldsymbol{\sigma}} = \Delta_{\max} + \ell s/2$，$c_{\ell,\boldsymbol{\sigma}} = \min_j(\Delta_j - \ell d s_{1j}/2)$。

- **定理1（logarithmic regret）**：当学习率满足 $0 < \ell < \min_j 2\Delta_j/(d s_{1j})$ 时，遗憾上界为 $O(\log T)$（有限时间、非渐近）。
